Our Story

Akilisoft Tech Company Limited is a software development company based in Dar es Salaam, Tanzania, focused on building mission-critical systems for government institutions and enterprise organizations. Our work spans govtech platforms, field data systems, and business applications — engineered to the reliability and security standards that public-sector deployments demand.

Our flagship engagement is a government-facing platform built for Tanzania's Ministry of TEHAMA (Wizara ya Mawasiliano na Teknolojia ya Habari), where we deliver systems designed for accountability, auditability, and long-term configurability rather than one-off builds.

This work is deliberately aligned with Tanzania's National Development Vision 2050 (Dira ya Taifa ya Maendeleo 2050) — in particular its pillars of Utawala Bora (Good Governance) and Mageuzi ya Kidijitali (Digital Transformation). We see software as infrastructure for national priorities, not just a service delivered to a client.
