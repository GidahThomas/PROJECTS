Company profile

Overview

Technology for institutions and businesses

Akilisoft Tech Company Limited builds software systems for government institutions and businesses in Tanzania. Its work includes govtech platforms, field data systems, and business applications.

Products and platforms

  • MFT — Mfumo wa Fuatilia Taarifa: citizen issue tracking and resolution; deployed for the Ministry of TEHAMA.
  • Sajili: offline-first field data capture for solar and off-grid energy programme teams.
  • Dubai Cargo System: cargo management and shipment tracking operations.
  • PMP — Project Management Portal: contact the company for confirmed capabilities, availability, and deployment details.

Services

Published services include web application development, mobile application development, CCTV camera installation, and systems consulting.

Leadership

Founder and CEO: Gidah Thomas, also known as Gidamasauda Thomas.
