About Us

Engineering trust into public and enterprise systems

Akilisoft Tech Company Limited is a software development company based in Dar es Salaam, Tanzania, focused on building mission-critical systems for government institutions and enterprise organizations. Our flagship engagement is a government-facing platform built for Tanzania's Ministry of TEHAMA, deliberately aligned with Tanzania's Dira ya Taifa ya Maendeleo 2050 (National Development Vision 2050).

Products

What does MFT do?

MFT (Mfumo wa Fuatilia Taarifa) supports tracking citizen-reported issues and their resolution. The platform is deployed for the Ministry of TEHAMA.

Can Sajili capture data when a field team is offline?

Sajili is described as an offline-first field data capture tool for solar and off-grid energy programme teams. Its records sync when connectivity returns.
