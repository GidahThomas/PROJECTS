About Us

Engineering trust into public and enterprise systems

Akilisoft Tech Company Limited is a software development company based in Dar es Salaam, Tanzania, focused on building mission-critical systems for government institutions and enterprise organizations. Our flagship engagement is a government-facing platform built for Tanzania's Ministry of TEHAMA, deliberately aligned with Tanzania's Dira ya Taifa ya Maendeleo 2050 (National Development Vision 2050).

Why Us

How we approach every deployment

Built for Institutions

Systems designed for the accountability, auditability, and security standards government and enterprise deployments demand. Role enforcement happens server-side, never trusted to the client.

Configurable From Day One

Branding, categories, departments, and workflows are configured, not rebuilt, so one proven deployment scales to new institutions without a rewrite.

Aligned With National Priorities

Government-facing work is built with Tanzania's Dira ya Taifa ya Maendeleo 2050 in mind, supporting Utawala Bora (Good Governance) and Mageuzi ya Kidijitali (Digital Transformation).

Built for Real Conditions

From offline-first field data capture to real-time dashboards, systems engineered for how Tanzania actually works, not just ideal conditions.
