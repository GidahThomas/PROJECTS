Digital services

Designing digital services around real public-service workflows

Digitising a public service is more than putting a form online. The system should reflect how a request moves through the institution: who receives it, who is responsible for the next action, what information is needed, and how progress is communicated.

Before implementation, map the current workflow with the staff who operate it and the people who use it. Identify handoffs, exceptions, escalation points, records that must be retained, and what users need to know at each step. Then agree on access roles and operational ownership.

For a citizen issue-tracking service such as MFT, a useful design goal is to make the status and responsibility of each case understandable. Performance measures should be based on agreed definitions and verified operational data, not illustrative figures.

Field operations

Planning field data collection for unreliable connectivity

Field teams cannot always assume a stable network. An offline-capable workflow should let staff capture and review records locally, clearly indicate what has synced, and retry synchronisation when a connection becomes available.

Plan for duplicate submissions, interrupted uploads, corrections, and device loss. Validate required fields on the device, protect locally stored information, and define how conflicting edits are resolved. Give field staff a clear way to identify records that still need attention.

Test the workflow in realistic connectivity conditions and with the devices field teams actually use. Sajili is described as an offline-first field data capture tool for solar and off-grid energy programme teams; detailed deployment requirements should be confirmed with the project team.

Product communication

Showing product interfaces honestly

A polished interface image can help explain a product, but a conceptual mockup is not evidence of a deployed feature. Label illustrative visuals clearly and distinguish them from screenshots captured from a live, authorised product environment.

When sharing real screenshots, remove personal or confidential data, obtain permission where required, and keep the image consistent with the currently available product. Explain which capabilities are generally available and which depend on a particular deployment.

This website labels conceptual product visuals as illustrations. Contact Akilisoft to discuss product capabilities and availability for a specific use case.
